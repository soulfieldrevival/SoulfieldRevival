About

Born in late-night solitude and rebuilt through rhythm, SoulField Revival is the rebirth of raw emotion through sound. Blending hip‑hop grit with blues soul and late‑night confessionals, it’s music that feels like a prayer whispered through static—the sound of a soul finding its way home. Created by independent artist Andrew Michael Lawrence, SoulField Revival isn’t a band—it’s a resurrection. Every song carries the weight of experience, forged from heartbreak, war, and revival. The beats hum like city neon, the lyrics cut like scripture, and every track invites you to step into the light after the storm. More than a project, SoulField Revival is a mission—to restore feeling in a world that’s gone numb.
